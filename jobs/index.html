How APS Jobs works

One list for every job, from first call to money in the bank.

  • Tap the status chip on a job to move it to the next step. Wrong tap? Hit Undo, or use Edit to pick any status.
  • Totals: enter materials and labour excl. VAT. The card shows VAT 15%, total and the deposit (default 80%).
  • WhatsApp: sends the scope and totals only — no fittings list.
  • Notes stay private and are never sent.
  • Saved on this phone only. Export CSV regularly as a backup, or to open in Excel. Import CSV to move jobs to another phone.
  • Install: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It then works without signal.
